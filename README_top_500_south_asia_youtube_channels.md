# Top 500 YouTube Channels in South Asia (2026): Most Popular YouTube Channels by Subscribers

> A South Asia-focused YouTube discovery resource built from a 500-channel subscriber-ranked CSV snapshot, with IndiTubeDb creator-profile links wherever a matching profile was located and verified.

## Discover better creators. Not just bigger creators.

YouTube in South Asia is no longer one single creator market. India, Bangladesh and Pakistan have developed enormous ecosystems across Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Odia, Bhojpuri and other languages. At the same time, global channels such as CoComelon, MrBeast 2, JustinBieberVEVO, Red Bull and Netflix India also appear in the supplied dataset.

That makes subscriber count useful for understanding scale—but not enough to answer a more important question: **which creator is right for you?**

This is where [IndiTubeDb](https://www.inditubedb.com/) fits into the discovery process. Instead of treating YouTube as one giant list, IndiTubeDb organizes creators around languages, categories, niches, geography and content characteristics, while also supporting creator comparisons, ratings, reviews, favorites, notes and AI-powered recommendations.

## What this 500-channel list contains

The list below is reproduced from the supplied CSV and preserves its ranking and channel names. It is a **snapshot**, not a live YouTube leaderboard. Subscriber numbers change continuously, so the ranking should be treated as a dataset snapshot rather than a permanent ranking.

The 500 names span:

- Music labels, artists and regional music channels
- Television networks and entertainment brands
- Gaming creators and gaming entertainment
- News, current affairs and commentary
- Education, competitive exams and learning
- Family vlogs, lifestyle and Shorts
- Kids, animation and nursery-rhyme channels
- Movies, trailers, dubbed cinema and film entertainment
- Food, cooking and village-life content
- Regional-language creators from across South Asia
- Large Pakistan- and Bangladesh-focused channels

## Why subscriber count is only the beginning

A channel with tens of millions of subscribers can be excellent for one viewer and completely irrelevant to another. A viewer looking for Bengali education content has a different discovery problem from someone looking for Punjabi music, Urdu drama, Tamil cooking, Hindi gaming or Malayalam family vlogs.

IndiTubeDb is designed around that distinction. Its current creator ecosystem supports structured discovery by language, category, niche and geography, alongside profile pages and comparison-oriented discovery. IndiTubeDb also publishes separate rankings for popularity and community ratings, making it possible to distinguish **reach** from **viewer evaluation**.

## South Asian YouTube is multilingual

One of the most important patterns visible in this dataset is that South Asian YouTube cannot be understood through Hindi and English alone. Regional-language ecosystems are major discovery markets in their own right.

For example, the dataset includes channels connected to Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Punjabi, Gujarati, Odia, Bhojpuri, Urdu and other audiences. IndiTubeDb's language-based directories make it possible to narrow discovery rather than forcing viewers to compete with every channel on one global popularity list.

The dataset also includes major Bangladeshi channels such as SOMOY TV, Rakib Hossain, Channel 24, SS FOOD CHALLENGE, Independent Television, Bongo, Prank King Entertainment, Holy Tune, Eagle Music Video Station, Sangeeta Music and CD Choice.

## Pakistan: Urdu entertainment, television and creator culture

## How to use this list with IndiTubeDb

The value of a large channel list is not simply knowing who has the most subscribers. It is using the list as a starting point for deeper discovery.

### 1. Discover by language

Start with a language instead of a subscriber count. Search for Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Odia, Bhojpuri and other supported languages to find creators who speak to the audience you actually care about.

### 2. Discover by category and niche

A viewer interested in gaming should not have to browse music labels and television networks. Likewise, someone researching education channels needs different discovery filters from someone looking for food, travel or movie content.

### 3. Compare creators

When several channels cover the same subject, compare them rather than relying only on subscriber count. IndiTubeDb's creator-comparison workflow is designed for exactly this kind of decision.

### 4. Use ratings and community reviews

Popularity measures reach. Community ratings and reviews can add another layer of context. IndiTubeDb maintains separate discovery surfaces for highly subscribed channels and highly rated channels, helping users understand that the biggest channel is not automatically the best fit.

### 5. Save creators and build a personal discovery library

For users who regularly research creators, favorites, notes and timestamp-based video bookmarking can turn a simple channel search into a personal YouTube research library.

### 6. Find creators beyond the obvious names

Large subscriber counts naturally dominate rankings. But regional-language and niche creators can be highly relevant even when they are much smaller. This is one of the biggest reasons to use structured creator discovery instead of a single popularity leaderboard.
